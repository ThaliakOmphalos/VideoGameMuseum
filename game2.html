Wuthering Waves

A world broken by catastrophe, a hero with no memory, and combat fast enough to feel like a fighting game. Wuthering Waves is all about motion.

About the Game

Wuthering Waves is a free-to-play open-world action RPG developed and published by Kuro Games, the studio behind Punishing: Gray Raven. It launched on PC, iOS, and Android on May 22, 2024. A PlayStation 5 version followed in January 2025, and the game later came to Steam and Mac. Progress syncs across platforms, so you can play on your phone and pick up later on a PC or console.

You play as the Rover, who wakes from a long sleep with no memory in a world that has been reshaped by a disaster called the Lament. Players explore regions full of ruins, cities, and strange creatures while teaming up with powerful allies called Resonators to uncover what happened.

Why It Belongs in the Museum

  • Combat that rewards skill. Dodging, parrying, and swapping characters at the right moment are just as important as your stats.
  • The Echo system. Defeated enemies can be absorbed as Echoes, which you can then summon in battle and use to customize your build.
  • Freedom of movement. Wall-running, grappling, and gliding make exploring the open world fast and fun.
  • A live, growing world. Regular version updates keep adding new regions, story chapters, and characters.

Why It's One of My Favorites

Wuthering Waves feels incredible to play. Fights are flashy and quick, and getting a perfect dodge into a character swap combo never gets old. Exploring is just as fun, since every cliff can be climbed and every ruin might hide a secret. Collecting Echoes from the enemies you defeat makes every battle feel worth it.

Every boss fight feels like learning a dance, and winning feels earned.
